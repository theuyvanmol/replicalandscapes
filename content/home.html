GOOD TO KNOW BEFORE YOU GROW

A little expert guidance.

Can you install artificial plants in commercial spaces?

Yes. We work on hospitality, office, retail and other commercial projects, as well as residential installations. Get in touch to discuss the site, the look you want to achieve and any access or fixing requirements.

Can artificial trees be made to suit a bespoke design?

We can build trees using real timber stems or manufactured trunks and create coordinated planting displays, hedges, green walls and foliage ceilings. Our team can advise on suitable materials and installation systems for your project.

Do you offer fire-retardant artificial foliage?

Inherently fire-retardant artificial foliage can be supplied where required. Tell us about your project’s requirements so the team can discuss suitable products and the relevant documentation before specification.

Do artificial plants need maintenance?

They do not need watering or growing conditions, but dust can build up on foliage. Our Leaf Clean department provides cleaning and maintenance for artificial tree displays. Ask us about the care your particular installation needs.
