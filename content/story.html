01 / OUR APPROACH

Artificial planting.
Real attention to detail.

We believe greenery should feel at home in a space, whether that space is a quiet living room or a busy restaurant.

Replica Landscapes brings together carefully selected artificial plants and trees with the expertise to design, build and install bespoke displays. With over 16 years of experience, we look beyond the first impression: the shape of a canopy, the character of a trunk and the way planting works with the room around it all matter.

Our collection offers an accessible starting point for homes and businesses. Our bespoke service takes that same attention to detail into projects where a particular scale, structure or finish is needed. Both share a focus on lifelike planting and practical solutions.

02 / BESPOKE & COMMERCIAL

From an idea
to an installation.

We have undertaken artificial plant installations across a variety of industries, with particular experience in hotels, restaurants, bars and offices. We also work on residential and smaller commercial projects, helping clients bring greenery into settings where limited sunlight or other practical constraints make living plants difficult.

Our work includes artificial trees with real timber stems and manufactured trunks, artificial boxwood and buxus hedges, replica green walls and foliage ceilings. For chains of shops, bars and restaurants, we can build coordinated displays that carry a consistent planting theme across multiple locations.

Where appropriate, a member of the team can visit the site to discuss installation requirements, access and suitable fixing systems. Inherently fire-retardant foliage can also be supplied where needed; the specification and supporting documentation should be discussed as part of the project brief.

03 / EXPERIENCE WITHOUT BOUNDARIES

Rooted in the UK.
Ideas that travel further.

Our artificial plant and tree installations have taken us throughout the UK, Europe and beyond. We have completed hundreds of foliage, hedge and ceiling supply-and-install projects, including work at height.

One particularly memorable project was a 15-metre gold-leaf apple tree for a shopping centre in Azerbaijan: an example of the scale and imagination a bespoke installation can bring to a setting. Each project has its own demands, and we draw on that varied experience to find a workable solution.

04 / LEAF CLEAN

Care that keeps
the character.

Good planting deserves attention long after installation.

Our Leaf Clean department offers artificial tree cleaning and maintenance to refresh established displays. Although artificial plants do not need watering, their foliage can collect dust over time. Regular care helps keep the display clean and its colours looking fresh.

During maintenance, the team may also identify branches or foliage that need replacing, helping you make informed decisions about the upkeep and useful life of your artificial trees. Speak to us about your display, its location and the type of maintenance required.
