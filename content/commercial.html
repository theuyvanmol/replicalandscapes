BESPOKE BUILD & INSTALLATION SERVICE

Your vision.
Room to grow.

Artificial plants and trees, designed to feel part of the architecture.

Replica Landscapes designs, builds and installs bespoke artificial planting for hospitality venues, workplaces and residential interiors. We work directly with clients and alongside interior designers, architects and fit-out contractors to turn an idea into a considered installation.

From a private home to a hotel refurbishment, new-build care home or large commercial interior, our approach starts with the setting. Scale, foliage, materials and positioning are considered together, so the finished planting complements the way the space is used.

01 / HOSPITALITY SPACES

A setting guests
remember.

Artificial planting for hotels, restaurants, bars and leisure venues.

Beautifully placed greenery can soften an interior, bring warmth and character, and make a venue feel more inviting. We create planting for hotel receptions, restaurant dining rooms, bars, cafés, spas, gyms and other leisure settings, shaped around the experience you want to create.

A statement tree, an artificial living wall, hanging foliage or a series of individual arrangements can each play a different role. Our team can design and manufacture pieces to complement your interior, working with you or your appointed design and fit-out team.

Artificial planting brings greenery throughout the year without watering or plant growth to manage. Cleaning and periodic checks still help keep the display looking its best.

02 / OFFICE & CORPORATE SPACES

A different kind
of working space.

Considered artificial plants for offices and shared workspaces.

Introduce greenery to reception areas, meeting rooms, breakout spaces and open-plan offices with planting that fits the layout. Artificial plants can bring visual softness to shelving, partitions and otherwise unused corners, including areas where light or access makes living plants harder to manage.

We can create coordinated plant and tree displays, planters, green walls and hanging schemes around your interior. Whether you are refreshing an existing office or planning a larger fit-out, we can work alongside your team to consider scale, placement and installation requirements.

The result is a more welcoming visual setting, with a consistent planting theme across the workplace.

These full-size artificial oak trees stand 6 metres tall and approximately 4 metres wide. Securely bolted to the floor, they bring the scale and character of mature trees into an interior, without the watering and growth management of living planting.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

A little clarity.
A better brief.

Which spaces do you design artificial planting for?

We work on hospitality venues, offices, residential interiors and care-home projects. We can discuss an individual feature or a coordinated planting scheme for a refurbishment or new build.

Can you work with our interior designer or contractor?

Yes. Our team can work directly with clients or alongside interior designers, architects and fit-out contractors to develop the planting and discuss the installation requirements.

Can artificial trees be made to a specific size?

Bespoke trees can be designed around the size, shape and foliage needed for a particular space. The 6-metre oak trees shown here are one example; access, structure and fixing requirements must be considered for each installation.

Is artificial planting completely maintenance-free?

No watering is needed, but dust can collect on foliage and installations benefit from appropriate care. Our Leaf Clean department offers artificial tree cleaning and maintenance; contact the team about your display.
