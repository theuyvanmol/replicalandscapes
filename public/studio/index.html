THE ROOM STUDIO

A space you can shape.

Add: tap a plant card. Move: drag a plant with your mouse or one finger. Drag the background to orbit the room; pinch or scroll to zoom. Adjust: select a plant and change its height, rotation, planter style, size and finish. The arrow buttons offer precise placement.

Save keeps your arrangement on this device. Up to 12 reference plants can be placed together. This is a 3D design demo, not a measurement or product-matching tool.

Live viewing requires WebGL2. If unavailable, a clearly labelled offline render of the same room is displayed, and editing controls are disabled.
